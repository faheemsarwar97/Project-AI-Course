## Presentation Tips

### Do's ✅
- Start with the problem, not the technology
- Show a live demo if possible
- Use visuals (diagrams, screenshots)
- Keep slides simple and focused
- Practice the demo workflow
- Prepare for common questions

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't overwhelm with technical details
- Don't skip the problem statement
- Don't forget to show the value proposition
- Don't rush through the demo
- Don't ignore limitations

### Timing Guide
- **Introduction**: 2-3 minutes
- **Problem & Solution**: 3-4 minutes
- **Technical Details**: 5-6 minutes
- **Demo**: 3-4 minutes
- **Future & Conclusion**: 2-3 minutes
- **Q&A**: 5-10 minutes

**Total**: ~20-30 minutes
